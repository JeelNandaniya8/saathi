Privacy

Your information should stay understandable and controllable.

This page explains what Saathi stores and which outside services process information. It avoids promises that no online service can honestly make.

Saathi is still being prepared for public launch. Do not store information you would not be comfortable keeping in an online account.

Information Saathi stores

  • Account details such as name, username, email, password hash, selected language and plan status. Verified Google sign-in can also save the Google profile photo URL. Your browser loads that image from Google; otherwise initials are shown.
  • Conversations, messages, the selected AI response mode and photos or PDFs attached to a message.
  • Tasks, reminders, habits, check-ins, journal entries and memories you choose to save.
  • Trusted-contact or Family features only after the required consent is provided.
  • Generated mock tests, submitted answers, scores and mindmaps; referral links and eligibility records; payment order metadata and access dates when applicable. Payment card and UPI credentials are handled by the payment provider, not stored by Saathi.
  • Limited security records used to prevent repeated login, signup and reset attempts.

Passwords are stored as hashes. Saathi should never ask you to send a password or verification code in a chat message.

Services that process information

Google Gemini

The Saathi server sends selected recent chat context, active memories and any photo or PDF attached to the current request to Google Gemini to prepare a reply. The browser does not receive the Gemini API key. Saathi may store provider-reported token counts, selected mode and attachment count to understand operating cost. This usage record does not make an extra copy of message or file content.

Google sign-in

When configured, Google provides a signed identity containing your account identifier, verified email and display name. Saathi verifies it on the server before creating or opening an account.

Razorpay

When checkout is enabled, Razorpay processes payment details. Saathi retains order references, amount, status and access dates to verify the purchase and handle refunds.

Brevo

Brevo processes the recipient name, email address and email content needed for verification, password reset and any separately enabled email delivery.

Study and wellbeing tools

Topics entered for mock tests and mindmaps are sent to Gemini. The wellbeing preparation guide is general: its private note is not stored or sent to an AI provider. Reminder suggestions use schedules you created; private journal and mood entries are not silently added to AI context. Scorecards include your name only when you choose it.

PostgreSQL hosting

The configured PostgreSQL provider stores account, workspace and attachment records. Hosting providers may process normal service logs needed to operate the application.

Browser drafts and reply timing

Unsent chat and Quick Note text can be stored in this browser under your account for recovery within 24 hours. Attachments are not copied into browser drafts. Expired drafts are removed when accessed. Signing out clears this account’s browser drafts; you can also clear them in Account settings.

Saathi records browser-measured reply durations, a conversation reference, outcome and error code without message or attachment contents. Your account keeps up to 100 timing records. The performance view shows the last 30 days; older records are removed when you view or record timings. You can export these records or clear them in Reply performance. Weekly review uses saved task, habit and practice activity without an extra AI request.

Private workspace boundaries

Journal entries, conversations, memories and check-ins belong to the signed-in account. Family features must not reveal private chats, journals, check-ins or memories to another person. A user must explicitly choose what to share.

Memory controls

Only memories the user saves are included in future Gemini context. Memories can be paused or deleted from the dashboard. Saathi does not claim to remember information outside the visible saved-memory list.

Quick notes

Quick notes are private to your account, included in your data export, and removed when you delete the note or account. They are not automatically sent to the AI or shared through Family Bridge.

Study spaces, revision and preferences

Subject spaces link your own saved chats, uploaded files, notes, tests and mindmaps. Removing a space removes its links, not the original items. Revision stores wrong test questions and flashcards you mark for another review, their answers, and review dates. These records, your language, goal, timezone and notification preferences are included in your export and deleted with your account. They are not automatically sent to AI or shared with contacts. Answer feedback stores your chosen rating against your message. Care plan changes happen only after you review and confirm the selected tasks.

Reminders and notifications

Reminder titles, notes, schedules and delivery status may be stored. Browser notification permission is controlled by the device. If you opt in to background alerts, Saathi stores your browser push endpoint and encryption keys and sends encrypted, generic alerts through the browser’s push service (such as Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft). Reminder titles and notes are not included in the push payload. Browser alerts can follow quiet hours or one daily summary in your saved timezone. Reminder emails keep their individually selected schedules. Disable alerts from Account settings or sign out to stop delivery on that device. Delivery depends on the configured scheduler, browser and operating system. Sensitive details should be kept out of lock-screen notifications where possible.

Optional personal context and exam plans

Care and study profile fields are stored separately from general memory, only after you confirm each field. You can edit or delete each field and stop all future profile use in AI. A separate, off-by-default permission lets a field be sent to the configured AI provider in the relevant mode: care fields only in Wellbeing, study fields only in supported study modes. Permission does not share anything with family or caregivers. Fields not reviewed for 90 days are excluded until reviewed again. Turning permission off cannot retract previously processed requests or saved replies; you can delete those conversations separately. These are user-reported details, not verified medical records.

Confirmed exam dates, topics and time budgets generate estimated study tasks only after you review and save a plan. Tasks remain editable in Planner. Removing a plan keeps its tasks. Profiles and plans are included in export and removed on account deletion.

Optional Google Classroom import

Classroom has a separate consent flow from Google login. If configured, Saathi requests read-only course and own-coursework access, then imports only courses you select. Tokens are encrypted on the server; they are excluded from account exports and never stored in the browser or sent to AI. Saathi does not import classmates or grades, turn in work, send teacher messages or automatically add assignments to AI context.

Manual sync, or a separately enabled six-hour sync with a configured protected server scheduler, records assignment titles, instructions, original links and published deadlines. Missing or uncertain deadlines stay unset. Information may be stale between syncs. Changing selected courses turns scheduled sync off until you review and enable it again. With separate permission for one selected assignment, its title, instructions and deadline can be sent to the configured AI provider to explain requirements and steps. No profile, memory or other assignments are included. The explanation is not stored as a conversation; usage counts are recorded. AI can misunderstand, so check the original. Adding an assignment to Planner creates a local task; marking it complete does not submit it in Classroom. Disconnect stops import and attempts to revoke access at Google. You can choose to remove imported records; existing Planner tasks remain. Account deletion removes stored connections and imports, but you should also remove Saathi access in your Google account.

Food choices and limited care sharing

The food organiser uses your entered choices, known ingredients, preferences and estimated costs without AI. It does not interpret clinical restrictions or certify allergy safety. Health-related choices require your report that a qualified clinician reviewed those exact options; that report is not verified. A separate confirmation stores the preview and any health restrictions you entered. Saved plans can be deleted and are included in export and account deletion.

When the clinical beta is enabled, an accepted trusted contact can receive a separate, limited request to read only selected medication schedules. Status and scheduled time are shared; names and exact instructions are optional. Both people must agree to the exact fields, and changes require fresh recipient acceptance. Access expires within 30 days, can be revoked by either person, and stops when the trusted-contact connection is removed. No automatic alerts, messages, emergency monitoring or caregiver editing are provided. A self-reported status is not proof of medication use. Consent metadata is visible to both people; records are read-only for the accepted recipient. Revocation cannot erase a recipient’s earlier copies.

Medication schedule log (gated private beta)

This optional feature is disabled until clinical and notification-delivery review. If enabled and you confirm a schedule, Saathi stores the medicine/schedule name, your exact entered instructions, timezone, recurrence and confirmation time. It records scheduled occurrences and your own taken, skipped or not-confirmed reports. It does not verify medication use, infer prescriptions, choose doses, notify caregivers or provide emergency monitoring. No medication-log content is automatically sent to AI. With separate permission, one prescription image or PDF can be sent to the configured AI provider for unverified transcription. Compare all names, numbers and units with the original and your clinician. Files and transcription drafts are not saved by this feature; usage counts are recorded. Nothing is automatically scheduled.

Snooze changes the notification time, not the saved schedule. Browser pushes use generic text and respect configured quiet hours and permissions. Delivery records show attempts and push-service acceptance, which do not prove receipt. After downtime, only the last 30 days are reconstructed. Editing a prescribed instruction currently requires deleting the old schedule and explicitly confirming a new one; deletion also removes its logs. You can pause alerts, correct records and export saved data. Account deletion removes these records.

Export, deletion and retention

A signed-in user can export account data and permanently delete the account. JSON exports list attachment metadata and signed-in download links; attachment files can be opened separately while the account exists. Deleting a message, conversation or account also deletes its active attachment records. Deleted data may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups before normal backup expiry.

Focus sessions and workspace search

Search reads only chats, notes and tasks in your account. Focus sessions save duration, elapsed time, status and an optional task link. We retain up to 100 sessions per account and include them in your data export. Deleting your account removes them. Closing the timer panel does not stop an active session.
