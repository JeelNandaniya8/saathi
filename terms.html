Terms

Simple rules for using Saathi responsibly.

These terms describe the current service in plain language. They do not turn planned features into promises.

Acceptable use

Use Saathi lawfully and respectfully. Do not try to access another person's account, overload the service, distribute malicious code or use Saathi to harm, deceive or exploit people.

AI limitations

Saathi uses AI and can be incomplete or wrong. Verify important information. Saathi is not a replacement for a qualified medical, mental-health, legal or financial professional and is not an emergency service.

Your responsibilities

  • Protect your password and verification codes.
  • Check reminder dates, time zones and repeat schedules.
  • Follow age requirements and involve a parent or guardian where required.
  • Only upload or save material you have permission to use.

Plans and payments

Free features may have fair-use limits. Plus and Family remain Coming Soon until a real payment system is connected and verified. Selecting an interest option does not create a paid subscription or charge money. Future paid terms, renewal rules, taxes and cancellation controls must be shown before checkout.

Availability and changes

The service can be interrupted by hosting, database, email, browser-notification or AI-provider failures. Features and limits may change, but Saathi should not quietly remove paid value during an active billing period.

Account removal

You can request permanent account deletion from account settings. Deletion cannot be undone after it completes.
