Care, without overstepping

Support should feel reassuring and stay honest.

Saathi can help with everyday planning and reflection. It is still AI, can make mistakes, and is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service.

Your account stays yours

Passwords are hashed and the AI provider key remains on your server.

Reminders are created by you

Saathi never selects a medicine or dose. It only follows the schedule you enter.

Limits stay visible

Important health, legal or financial information should be checked with a qualified person.
