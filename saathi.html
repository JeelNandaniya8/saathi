The thing no one else does

Saathi checks on you, like a person would.

Most AI waits for you to open the app. Saathi does not. It remembers your routine and reaches out first, especially built for people who need a little extra looking after, like a parent living alone.

  • Daily medicine and routine reminders, with a real follow up if you do not reply
  • Mood check ins that build a gentle picture over weeks, not just one chat
  • An optional Family Bridge, where a trusted person is notified only if check ins are missed repeatedly
  • Always transparent. Saathi points toward real doctors and professionals, and never pretends to be one
