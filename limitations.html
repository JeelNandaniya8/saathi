AI limitations

Helpful does not mean infallible.

Saathi should be useful without pretending to understand, monitor or guarantee more than it really can.

Saathi is AI

Replies are generated by an AI model. Saathi does not have human judgment, personal experience or awareness. It can misunderstand context and produce confident mistakes.

Verify important information

Check information that could affect health, safety, education, money, law or another important decision with a reliable source or qualified person.

Photo and PDF limits

Saathi can miss handwriting, diagrams, small text, page order or context in an uploaded file. File analysis may be incomplete or wrong. Check the original material and do not rely on an AI summary as a substitute for reading important documents.

Not professional or emergency support

Saathi is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer, financial adviser or emergency service. It does not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee wellbeing or contact help on a user's behalf.

Reminder limits

A reminder can fail because the browser is closed, permission is denied, the device is offline, the server is unavailable or the schedule is wrong. Users should keep another reliable method for anything important.

Family boundaries

Family features are coordination tools, not monitoring. They do not prove that someone is safe or that a task was completed. Private conversations and journals are not shared by default.

Memory is user controlled

Only visible active memories are intentionally reused. Saved memory can still be incomplete, outdated or irrelevant and should be reviewed regularly.
